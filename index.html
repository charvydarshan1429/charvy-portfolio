01 / About me

A little more than a résumé

An engineer with a creative side.

I'm Kaila Charvy, an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at NIT Nagaland, currently building my foundation across electrical engineering, programming, robotics and emerging technology.

I enjoy understanding how things work and then getting my hands on them — whether that's a circuit, a motor-driven project, a simulation, a data pipeline or a cybersecurity problem. I'm especially drawn to projects where hardware and software meet.

Beyond academics, I'm naturally creative and socially active. I enjoy communication, writing, speaking, visual work and taking responsibility in team environments. My school years included awards in story writing, essay writing, speech and debate, and animation, which shaped the communication and creative side I bring into engineering.

Right now, I'm focused on becoming a well-rounded engineer — technically curious, practical, collaborative and always willing to learn something outside the obvious path.
